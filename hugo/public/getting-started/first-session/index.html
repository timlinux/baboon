Your First Session

A walkthrough of your first Baboon typing session

Let’s walk through your first complete typing session with Baboon.

Starting the App

Launch Baboon from your terminal:

baboon

You’ll see the typing screen with a word displayed in large block letters.

The Typing Phase

Understanding the Display

  • Header: Shows “BABOON - Typing Practice” and word progress (e.g., “Word 1/30”)
  • Main Area: The current word in large block letters
  • Footer: WPM bar and keyboard hints

Typing Your First Word

  1. Look at the word displayed
  2. Type each letter - watch them turn green as you go
  3. If you make a mistake, the letter turns red
  4. Use Backspace to correct errors
  5. When done, press Space to advance

Visual Feedback

Letters change colour as you type:

  • Gray: Not yet typed
  • Orange: Current position (your cursor)
  • Green: Correctly typed
  • Red: Incorrectly typed

The Results Screen

After completing 30 words, you’ll see your results:

Core Statistics

  • WPM (Words Per Minute): Your typing speed
  • Accuracy: Percentage of correct keystrokes
  • Time: How long the round took

Each stat shows:

  • Your current score
  • Your personal best
  • Your average across all sessions
  • A ⭐ if you set a new personal best!

Advanced Statistics

  • Finger Accuracy: How accurate each finger is
  • Row Accuracy: Performance on top, home, and bottom rows
  • Hand Balance: Left vs. right hand usage
  • Rhythm: Consistency of your typing speed
  • Common Errors: Letters you frequently mistype

Letter Statistics

A grid showing all 26 letters with colour-coded indicators for:

  • Accuracy: How often you type each letter correctly
  • Frequency: How often each letter appears
  • Speed: How fast you type each letter

Starting a New Round

Press Enter or Tab to start another round.

Tips for Your First Sessions

  1. Focus on accuracy first - Speed will come naturally
  2. Don’t look at the keyboard - Trust your muscle memory
  3. Use the home row - Keep fingers on ASDF and JKL;
  4. Relax your hands - Tension slows you down
  5. Take breaks - Don’t over-practice in one session

Building Good Habits

Baboon tracks your weak letters and prioritises them in future rounds. This means:

  • The more you practice, the more targeted your practice becomes
  • Your accuracy for problem letters will improve over time
  • Eventually, all letters will become equally accurate
